ExoTraj: A General Lower-limb Exoskeleton Assistance Policy for Complex Environments

Xiao-Yin Liu, Guotao Li*, Long Sun, Xu Liang, Zeng-Guang Hou*
Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences
*Indicates Corresponding Authors
Page Code (Coming Soon)

Movie 1: The assistance performance of ExoTraj in complex outdoor environments. ExoTraj policy performs real-time trajectory prediction and torque optimization. It supports a range of assistance tasks, including walking on uneven grass, climbing rocky slopes, variable-speed running, obstacle crossing, and sudden stops and starts. The policy can be adapted to high-dynamic tasks, delivering real-time and efficient assistance.
